Inside a Kubernetes Service

A Service's ClusterIP belongs to no machine. kube-proxy on every node turns the Service's list of ready pods (its EndpointSlice) into rules that rewrite each new connection to one pod. Step through to follow single connections, and see what happens when pods stop being ready, shut down, or get added.
