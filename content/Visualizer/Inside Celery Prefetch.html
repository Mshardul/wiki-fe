Inside Celery Prefetch

A Celery task is a message. Two moments decide most surprises: when a worker reserves it (takes it into its own buffer) and when the worker acknowledges it (lets the broker forget it). Until it's acknowledged, the broker can hand it out again. Step through to watch messages move between the broker and the workers.
