### Design patterns & coupling

#### [MINOR] `section-map.js` and `link-graph.js` overlays bypass `modal-registry.js` entirely

- **Concern:** design-patterns
- **Prior:** new
- **Files:** `js/app/section-map.js` (no `registerModal`, no Escape-key handler, no focus-trap call), `js/app/link-graph.js` (same)
- **Observation:** every other modal-shaped controller (`complexity-compare.js`, `bookmarks-modal.js`, `wiki-switcher.js`, `auth.js`, `search.js`, `settings-theme.js`) calls `registerModal` from `js/modal-registry.js`. `section-map.js` and `link-graph.js` only wire a click-outside-to-close backdrop listener (`document.getElementById(...-backdrop/-overlay).addEventListener("click", ...)`) — no Escape-key close, no focus trap, no registry entry at all.
- **Impact:** these two overlays are keyboard-inaccessible to dismiss (no Escape) and don't participate in whatever close-stack/focus-restore behavior the registry centralizes for every other modal; a user who opens the section-map or link-graph overlay and presses Escape gets no response, unlike every other modal in the app.
- **Fix direction:** route both through `registerModal`, or if they're deliberately excluded (e.g. because they're canvas overlays, not dialogs), document that exception near their open functions.

### Module-boundary contract

#### [MAJOR] `render/home-index.js` owns a localStorage key (`wiki-pinned-wikis`) with its own getter/setter, and `storage/data-clear.js` has to import back into `render/` to clear it

- **Concern:** module-boundaries
- **Prior:** new
- **Files:** `js/render/home-index.js:32-49` (`PINNED_WIKIS_KEY`, `getPinnedWikis`, `setPinnedWikis`), `js/storage/data-clear.js:1,65` (`import { setPinnedWikis } from "../render/home-index.js"`)
- **Observation:** CONVENTIONS: "`js/storage/` owns localStorage. No other module touches `localStorage` directly." `home-index.js` is a `render/` file, not `storage/`, yet defines and owns a full localStorage key lifecycle (read, write, and an exported mutator). The layering inversion is visible directly in the codebase: `storage/data-clear.js` — which should be the *authority* clearing storage keys — has to reach into `render/home-index.js` to call `setPinnedWikis([])`, i.e. the storage-domain clear path depends on a render-domain module for a storage operation.
- **Impact:** the next person adding a new storage key modeled on "pinned wikis" as precedent will keep placing storage logic in `render/`; the data-clear/wipe path is harder to audit for completeness (Concern 10) because one of its localStorage owners lives outside the domain folder whose whole job is being the wipe-path's single source of truth.
- **Fix direction:** move `PINNED_WIKIS_KEY`/`getPinnedWikis`/`setPinnedWikis` into a `js/storage/` module (e.g. a new small file, or fold into `bookmarks.js`-adjacent "home preferences" file), have `home-index.js` import the accessor instead of owning it, and have `data-clear.js` import from `storage/` like every other clear step.
